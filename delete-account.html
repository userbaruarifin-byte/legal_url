Delete your Monthli account

Monthli, by FinProject

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Monthli and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Data & privacy.
  3. Optional: tap Export backup (.json) first if you want to keep a copy of your books.
  4. Tap Delete account and local data, then Delete account to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Can't open the app?

Email the support address shown on Monthli's Google Play page from the email address you signed up with, and ask for your account to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Monthli account: email address, sign-in identifiers, name and profile.
  • Your trial and subscription record on the Monthli server.
  • When you delete in the app, the books stored on your phone (transactions, receipts, reports) are deleted too. They were never uploaded to us.

What may be kept

  • Short-lived server logs (method, address and status only, no financial data) for up to 30 days.
  • Purchase records that Google Play and RevenueCat must keep by law. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

See also the Privacy Policy.
