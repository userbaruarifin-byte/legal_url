Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Monthli is a local-first bookkeeping app. Your transactions, categories, receipts, tax settings, invoices, assets, liabilities and reports are stored on your device. Signing in does not upload them.

On Android, if your phone's own Google backup is turned on, it keeps an encrypted copy of your bookkeeping records (not receipt photos or sign-in tokens) in your Google account so you can restore them on a new phone. Monthli cannot read that copy.

Account and subscription data

When you create an account, Supabase processes your email address, sign-in identifiers, session data and basic profile data to create and secure the account. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and name with us for this purpose. Subscription status is processed by RevenueCat and the app store. These records are linked to your account, not to your on-device books.

Optional AI features

AI features are optional. Only when you start one does Monthli send the summary or goal text shown on screen to the Monthli server, which checks your subscription and forwards the request to the AI provider. In the AI chat, the text you type, the photos you attach, your category names and today's date are sent to Groq, Inc. on servers in the United States; Monthli does not store them, and Groq does not keep them by default. Your full local database is never sent.

Ads

Users without Monthli Pro see a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google collects your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions under the Google partner sites policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Ad events (shown, clicked, estimated ad revenue) are also recorded by RevenueCat to report app revenue. Your books, amounts and receipts are never shared with ad networks. Monthli Pro has no ads.

Device permissions

Camera and photo access is used only when you attach a receipt. The microphone is used only when you tap "Say it instead" to enter a record by voice: your phone's own speech service (on Android usually Google's, which may process it online under that provider's own privacy terms) turns it into text, Monthli only receives that text and stores no audio. Notifications are used only for reminders you turn on. Biometric unlock is handled by your phone; Monthli never receives fingerprints or face data.

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device stays until you delete it, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or restore another backup. Check how long you are legally required to keep bookkeeping records before deleting them. In Settings → Data & privacy you can export a backup and permanently delete your account together with your local data. Account and subscription records are deleted with the account, except short-lived infrastructure logs and records the app store or payment provider must keep by law.

Security

Monthli uses the phone's app storage, encrypted connections (TLS), account sign-in, database access rules, optional biometric unlock and a salted PIN. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your own backup.

Children

Monthli is a business tool and is not intended for children.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the effective date above is updated. Questions or deletion requests: use the support contact listed on the Monthli app store page.
